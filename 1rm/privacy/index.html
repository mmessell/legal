1RM Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

1RM is an iPhone app for tracking your one-rep max and training percentages. It is built so that your data stays yours.

The short version

  • We do not collect any personal data.
  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • Your workouts are stored on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud, which we cannot access.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple.

Data stored by the app

The exercises, workouts and sets you log, and your app settings (such as units and theme), are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also stored in your private iCloud account so they sync between your devices. This uses Apple's iCloud (CloudKit private database). Only you can access this data; the developer has no access to it and runs no servers that receive it.

Purchases

1RM Pro is sold through the App Store as an auto-renewable subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Apple processes the payment and tells the app only whether you have 1RM Pro. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.

Data we collect

None. The app sends no data to the developer or to third parties, and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

If you choose to email feedback (for example with Send Feedback in the app), we receive your email address, your message and the app, iOS and iPhone model details included in it. We use them only to reply and to improve 1RM, never share them, and delete them on request.

Deleting your data

You can delete exercises and workouts in the app. To remove all 1RM data from iCloud, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage (or Manage Account Storage), choose 1RM and delete its data. Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
