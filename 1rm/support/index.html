1RM Support

Help with the 1RM iPhone app

Common questions

My lifts don't appear on my other iPhone

1RM syncs through your own iCloud. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, then open the app on both. In 1RM, Settings › About › iCloud Sync shows whether sync is on.

How is my one-rep max calculated?

Your 1RM is the heavier of the heaviest weight you've lifted and, if "Estimate from reps" is on, the best estimate from a formula (Epley by default) using sets of 2 or more reps. You can change the formula and the time range in Settings › One-rep max.

Why are the percentages rounded?

Percentages round to weights you can put on a bar (2.5 kg or 5 lb by default). Change the step, your bar and your plates in Settings › Units.

I bought 1RM Pro on another device

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancelling 1RM Pro

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
